## Consistency checks and saved calculations

[check_human_audit.py](../tools/check_human_audit.py) reproduces the 12 saved comparison rows in [AGREEMENT_METRICS.csv](data/final_comparison/AGREEMENT_METRICS.csv): intermediate archived-reference, final-adjudicated and extraction-aware scenarios, each under strict, primary, secondary and either-judge flags. It excludes human Cannot assess and all targeted cases. Unsupported/Contradicted are human positives; automated CONTRADICTED/NOT_IN_SOURCES are positive flags. Strict UNCERTAIN is unflagged, not affirmative support.

The final random sample is 32 responses / 313 assessable claims: 297 Supported and 16 Unsupported. Strict consensus has TP=2, FP=8, TN=289, FN=14, agreement 0.9297124601, precision 0.2, recall 0.125 and Cohen's κ 0.1192120747. The targeted 120 claims have 114 Supported, three Unsupported and three Cannot assess. Totals are 433/411/19/0/3 as in the manuscript. These are sampled measurement diagnostics, not human-corrected full-study endpoints or human-confirmed strategy rankings.

The [extraction triage](data/adjudicated_review/EXTRACTION_TRIAGE.csv) preserves six response-level clear-rewrite, four possible-rewrite and one potential-omission classifications. The [36 QA flags — original path `data/adjudicated_review/CLAIM_QA_FLAGS.csv`; withheld](../../rights/WITHHELD_FILES.csv) are review items, not 36 demonstrated failures. Separately, the final-comparison claim table flags seven claim-level confirmed distortions: six random and one targeted. Removing the six random claims reproduces the saved exploratory N=307 strict matrix (TP=1, FP=7, TN=289, FN=10), without changing original judgments or the 800-response endpoint. [Original extraction notes](data/initial_review/EXTRACTION_AUDIT.csv), final dispositions and repeat rationales stay separate.

The saved full-800 four-rule sensitivity means and 5,000-resample intervals are later supplementary automated-label analyses, preserved as worksheet exports, not new inference performed for this appendix. Their original bootstrap script was not located; no exact interval reproduction is asserted. Shared extraction, a single reviewer, response/task clustering, uncertain extraction fidelity and selection of challenging cases limit interpretation. This audit does not systematically validate RQ2 citation mismatch, population-level judge accuracy or complete extraction recall.
