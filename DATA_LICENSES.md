## Reproduced text and adaptations

A raw dataset grant and a file's format do not settle every component of a processed task, rendered request, model output, judge log or human-review extract. Apply the original source conditions to identifiable protected copied/adapted material; adding model text or annotations does not remove them. Database and contents rights remain separate. Conversely, the existence of a source fact, citation or short matching phrase is not itself a legal determination of copyright protection or substantiality.

For HaluEval-derived records, the applicable CC BY-SA obligations concern protected HotpotQA/Wikipedia material and, where §4 applies, qualifying derived databases. They are not automatically extended to unrelated software or figures. MIT remains limited to the listed original software. The source task/packet adaptation processes are described in the original Code guide; no experimental file was rewritten for licensing.

The previous Hugging Face SciFact card's cc-by-nc-2.0 label and S2ORC's old-versus-current release history are recorded in [DATA_ORIGINS.json](DATA_ORIGINS.json). The direct SciFact component licence is the verified upstream statement for this inspection; conflicting metadata is not silently converted into a universal abstract-content grant.

Exact source hashes, pinned revisions and canonical publication citations are in DATA_ORIGINS.json and [DATASET_CITATIONS.bib](DATASET_CITATIONS.bib). Attribution alone is not permission. The original benchmark workbook and duplicate raw SciFact tar are excluded as approved, while included extracted claim/annotation files remain intact and the abstract corpus is withheld.
